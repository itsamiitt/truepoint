# START HERE — The Complete Plan at a Glance

## What this business is

We sell **live B2B data about companies and people through an API**, priced with credits (pay for what you use). Our customers are **software companies and AI-agent builders**, not individual sales reps.

Think of it this way: ZoomInfo and Apollo sell a *tool* to salespeople. We sell the *fuel* (data) to the companies building the next generation of sales, recruiting, and investing software.

**One line:** "Real-time company and people data, delivered by API, priced by usage — built for AI agents and the products that use them."

## Why this can work

1. **We already have the machine.** Our data pipeline (procured → QC → qualified → packaged), the enrichment waterfall (Crustdata + Sales Navigator fallback), the Truepoint contacts database, and the contributor network idea are all pieces of this business. We are productizing what we already built for ourselves.
2. **The market is big and growing.** The sales intelligence market is worth roughly **$4–5 billion in 2026** and is growing at 11–15% per year. Pay-as-you-go pricing is the fastest-growing pricing model in the category (~18% yearly growth).
3. **The timing is right.** AI agents are becoming the main consumers of B2B data. Old seat-based pricing (pay per human user) does not work for software that makes thousands of calls a day. Usage pricing does. Crustdata proved this model: ~20 people, profitable, 150+ customers.
4. **India cost advantage.** The expensive part of a data business is quality control and engineering. Our cost for both is a fraction of a US competitor's cost.

## The plan in 4 phases

| Phase | Months | What we do | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Sell flat files | 0–4 | Sell monthly-refreshed CSV datasets from our existing pipeline | First revenue, proof people will pay |
| 2. Launch the API | 3–8 | Wrap our waterfall in our own credit-priced API + MCP server | Recurring usage revenue |
| 3. Own the supply | 6–18 | Contributor network + our own crawling replace vendor data | High margins, real moat |
| 4. Win the agent market | 9–24 | Watchers (webhooks), signals, India/APAC coverage | Differentiation and lock-in |

## Target numbers (base case, end of month 18)

- 40–60 paying customers
- ~$25,000–$40,000 monthly recurring revenue (MRR)
- Gross margin above 70% (gross margin = revenue minus the direct cost of delivering the data)
- Profitable from roughly month 10–12 because our costs stay small

These are working targets, not promises. The financial file (09) shows the assumptions behind them and a slower scenario too.

## The one rule that decides everything

**Do not stay a reseller.** If we only resell Crustdata/Apollo data, we have a low-margin arbitrage business that a vendor policy change can kill overnight. Every month, more of the data we sell must come from sources we own (contributed data + our own crawling). That shift is what turns this into a real data company.
