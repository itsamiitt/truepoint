# Segments & Beachhead

## The density law
A contributor network's value is a function of **corroboration density inside a
niche**, not total breadth. One niche where every record has five recent
touches beats ten niches of stale singletons — because the freshness cluster
(S-08/09/10/13) is only winnable where enough contributors overlap on the same
accounts. Therefore: pick one beachhead where (a) incumbent coverage is
visibly weak, (b) sellers cluster in communities you can reach, and (c) your
own founding team has distribution. Expand niche-by-niche, never "everyone."

## Candidate beachhead profile (choose in Phase 0; record in decisions.md)
- A vertical+geo where incumbent data is thin (e.g., mid-market companies in a
  region incumbents under-cover, or a specific industry with churn-heavy
  titles). Selection criteria, scored per candidate: incumbent coverage gap ·
  reachable seller community · account overlap among sellers (density
  potential) · willingness to pay · compliance complexity of the geo.

## Provisional segment hypotheses (validate in Phase 0)
1. **Quota-pressured SDRs at SMB/mid-market vendors selling into the
   beachhead niche.** Underserved on: S-04, S-08, S-09, S-03. Price-sensitive
   → the Free + Community tiers (C-03) are the acquisition wedge. v1 hero user.
2. **Solo founders / first sales hires.** Same outcomes, zero budget, high
   contribution willingness. Community seeders; noisy advocates.
3. **RevOps at 20–200-rep orgs.** Underserved on: S-15, S-07, A-01, A-02,
   S-11. They don't prospect; they buy. Phase 4–5 expansion target — CRM-sync
   value lands here even if reps never open the app.
4. **Recruiters.** Near-identical job map; a later lateral market, not v1.

## Consumption-chain jobs that gate adoption (not core-job outcomes, still fatal)
- Extension install must survive IT review → minimal permissions (08, 09).
- First reveal must succeed within minutes of signup on the user's OWN niche
  (seed data strategy in 07 exists to guarantee this).
- Tier structure must be legible in 30 seconds — what's free, what Community
  adds and what keeps it active, what's paid. No fine print.
