# Desired Outcomes

Syntax: [Minimize|Increase] + [time / likelihood / number] + object + context.
Tracks: S- seller · C- contributor · A- admin/network health · X- reference
outcomes we track only to prove they're overserved (non-goals).
Status: all statements below are HYPOTHESES generated analytically on ⟨date⟩,
pending Phase 0 interviews. Revise here first, then rescore in 04.
C-03/C-04 were redefined on ⟨date⟩ for the freemium decision — see decisions.md.

## S — Seller outcomes (by job step)

Define
- S-01: Minimize the time it takes to translate ICP criteria into a reusable,
  searchable definition.

Locate
- S-02: Increase the likelihood that people matching the ICP at a target
  account can be found at all (coverage in the seller's niche).
- S-03: Minimize the time it takes to obtain a work email for a specific
  person identified elsewhere (e.g., while viewing their profile).
- S-04: Increase the likelihood of obtaining a direct-dial or mobile number
  that actually connects to the intended person.
- S-05: Increase the number of contacts available to browse overall.
  [expected overserved — the "recipe library" of this market]

Prepare
- S-06: Minimize the time it takes to move a found contact into the CRM or a
  list without retyping any field.
- S-07: Minimize the likelihood of creating a duplicate CRM record when
  adding a contact.

Confirm
- S-08: Minimize the likelihood that an email bounces on first contact.
- S-09: Minimize the likelihood that a record's person has left the company
  or changed roles by the time they are contacted.
- S-10: Minimize the time it takes to determine how recently, and on how much
  evidence, a record was last verified.
- S-11: Minimize the likelihood of contacting a person on a suppression,
  opt-out, or do-not-call list.

Execute
- S-12: Minimize the number of tools required between finding a person and
  initiating contact. [integration, not a sequencer — see non-goals]

Monitor
- S-13: Minimize the time between a saved contact changing jobs and the
  seller learning about it.

Modify
- S-14: Minimize the time it takes to replace a departed contact with the
  current holder of that role.

Conclude
- S-15: Minimize the ongoing effort required to keep CRM records current
  after outreach cycles.

## C — Contributor outcomes
- C-01: Minimize the marginal effort required to make a contribution while
  doing normal sales work. [north star of network design]
- C-02: Minimize the risk that contributing reveals the contributor's target
  accounts, contacts, or pipeline to competitors or anyone else.
- C-03: Increase the likelihood that keeping a contribution channel active
  visibly pays for itself in expanded free access (Community tier).
- C-04: Minimize the time between activating a contribution channel and
  receiving the expanded access.
- C-05: Increase the contributor's confidence that what they are sharing is
  lawful for them to share.
- C-06: Minimize the likelihood of being penalized for a good-faith
  contribution that turns out to be wrong.

## A — Admin, compliance & network-health outcomes
- A-01: Increase the likelihood that every stored field carries documented
  provenance and a lawful basis for processing.
- A-02: Minimize the time it takes to honor an opt-out or erasure request
  across the entire graph and all exports.
- A-03: Minimize the likelihood that fabricated or low-quality contributions
  enter the graph (contribution-fraud defense, incl. Community-status gaming).
- A-04: Minimize the likelihood that two records describing the same person
  diverge (entity-resolution integrity).

## X — Reference outcomes (tracked to justify non-goals)
- X-01: Improve multi-step outreach sequencing/cadence execution.
- X-02: Reduce the effort of writing outreach copy (AI writing).
- X-03: = S-05 (raw database size).
- X-04: Increase the likelihood of knowing which accounts are in-market
  (intent data). [genuinely valuable; deferred — not our wedge, and it
  requires scale we don't have yet. Revisit after Phase 5.]
