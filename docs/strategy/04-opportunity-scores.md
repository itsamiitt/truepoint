## Reading the landscape
The entire top of the board is one theme: **freshness and trust** — decay
detection (S-09, S-13, S-14), reachability of hard fields (S-04, S-08),
transparent confidence (S-10), and the network mechanics that make those
possible at all (C-01, C-02, A-03, A-01). The bottom of the board is where
incumbents advertise: size, sequencing, AI writing. Strategy: **differentiated
play on the freshness cluster, powered by the contributor network, with
compliance as the enterprise unlock; freemium pricing, with a Community tier
that ties expanded free access to keeping the network fed.**

## Current non-goals (do not invest; hold the line)
- S-05 database size: buy/license the baseline, never compete on the number.
- X-01/X-02 sequencing & writing: crowded, overserved; integrate instead
  (S-12 is met by connectors, not by building a sequencer).
- X-04 intent: revisit only after the flywheel produces the necessary scale.
