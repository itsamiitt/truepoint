# Job Map — "Build and maintain an accurate, reachable list of the right
# people to contact at target accounts" (SELLER)

The fourth column is this product's special sauce: for each step, the
contribution exhaust that step can ethically yield. Consumption and supply are
the same motion seen from two sides.

| # | Step | Seller is trying to… | Typical struggles / workarounds | Our boundary | Contribution exhaust available here |
|---|------|----|----|----|----|
| 1 | Define | Pin down ICP: titles, seniority, industry, geo, company size, tech stack, territory rules | ICP lives in a doc nobody re-reads; filters don't match how they think | Assist (saved ICPs) — meet-bar | ICP definitions = anonymized demand signal for which segments need coverage |
| 2 | Locate | Find matching accounts and people; get a work email/phone for a specific person found elsewhere (usually LinkedIn) | Tab-juggling; guessing email patterns; incumbent has no data for their niche | Execute for them (search + extension reveal) | **Reveal-misses** = precise map of missing/weak records → drives the most-wanted feed and crawl targeting |
| 3 | Prepare | Move found people into CRM/sequence without retyping; avoid creating duplicates | Copy-paste; CSV wrangling; dupes multiply | Execute for them (one-click save w/ dedupe) | Save events confirm person↔company↔role linkage; dedupe decisions improve entity resolution |
| 4 | Confirm | Verify email validity, number reachability, person still in role, not on a suppression/DNC list, before spending effort | No way to know record age; burn discovers decay | Execute for them (verification engine + confidence badge) | **Every verification a user runs updates the shared graph** (status + timestamp, never message content) |
| 5 | Execute | Run the outreach (their sequencer/dialer/inbox) | — | **Leave alone** (non-goal: X-01) — integrate, don't compete | Opt-in mailbox/dialer metadata: bounce events invalidate emails; connects validate phones (metadata only, never content) |
| 6 | Monitor | Notice decay: bounces, "no longer here" replies, job changes among saved contacts | Discovery-by-burn; LinkedIn stalking | Execute for them (job-change + decay alerts) | Change detections corroborated across many users' saved lists |
| 7 | Modify | Replace departed contacts; find the successor; fix wrong fields | Manual re-research; wrong data lingers in CRM forever | Execute for them (successor suggestions; one-tap corrections) | **Corrections at the moment of pain** — the highest-signal contribution type there is |
| 8 | Conclude | Push clean records back to CRM; report list health; refresh for next cycle | CRM rots; nobody owns hygiene | Execute for them (two-way sync, health dashboard) | CRM sync deltas (field-level, anonymized, contributor-controlled) = bulk freshness channel |

Note: this maps the seller's JOB, not our product or the purchase journey.
The purchase/setup/admin-approval experience is a consumption-chain job handled
in 05-segments.md and 09-compliance.md.
